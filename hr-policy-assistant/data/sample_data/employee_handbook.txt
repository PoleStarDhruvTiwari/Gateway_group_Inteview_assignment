EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK - HR POLICIES
Version 2024.1

WORK HOURS
Standard work hours: 9am to 5pm local time, Monday through Friday
Flexible scheduling available with manager approval
Overtime must be pre-approved by department head

BENEFITS SUMMARY
Health Insurance: Medical, dental, vision coverage available
401(k): Company matches 100% up to 4% of salary
Paid Time Off: 15 days vacation, 10 sick days, 11 holidays

CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees must:
- Maintain confidentiality of sensitive information
- Avoid conflicts of interest
- Report any ethical concerns to HR
- Follow all company policies and procedures

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Quarterly check-ins with manager
Annual performance review in December
Merit increases effective January 1
