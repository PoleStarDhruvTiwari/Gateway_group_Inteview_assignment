PARENTAL LEAVE POLICY
Last Updated: March 15, 2024

MATERNITY LEAVE
- Primary caregivers: 12 weeks paid leave
- Additional 4 weeks unpaid
- Must take within 3 months of birth/adoption
- State benefits may supplement in CA, NY, NJ

PATERNITY LEAVE
- 4 weeks paid leave
- Must be taken within 6 months
- Flexible scheduling available

ADOPTION LEAVE
- Same as maternity/paternity based on primary/secondary caregiver status
- Additional $5000 adoption assistance

STATE-SPECIFIC BENEFITS
California: Up to 8 weeks Paid Family Leave (supplemental)
New York: 12 weeks paid at 67% salary (state program)
New Jersey: 12 weeks paid at 85% (up to cap)

RETURN TO WORK
- Phased return option: 3 weeks at 50% time
- Lactation accommodation available
- Parental support groups
